The ARIA Assessment: take stock of the AI you already use.

List the AI tools your team relies on, then work through four stages for each one: what it does, how it fails, what you think of it, and when you’ll look again. You finish with a one-page summary you can print.

Your answers are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What ARIA is

Four stages, in order.

  1. Audit. Write down every AI system you actually touch, including the ones that came bundled with other software, and name one person who owns each.
  2. Risk and reliability. For each system, work out how it fails, whether you’d notice, and what a missed failure would cost.
  3. Informed position. State what you think of each system: where it helps, where it’s risky, the conditions for using it, and what you don’t yet know.
  4. Adaptive maintenance. Decide what would make you look again, who checks, and how often.

An honest “we don’t know” is more useful here than a guess that looks complete. If you’d rather do this with someone, that’s the Stocktake.
