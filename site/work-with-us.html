For: Leadership teams being asked “what’s our AI plan?” without one.

Format: In person or video. Fixed fee: [price, or from $X].

The Stocktake

An AI audit of your business, in plain English.

[2 to 3] weeks. What AI your people already use, approved or not; what it costs; where it would save real money, task by task; and where you’re exposed through data, contracts or regulation. You get a ranked list of where AI fits, what each move costs, and the order to do them in.

For: Companies that need a plan they can defend to a board.

The Bill

An AI cost check before you sign.

Before you sign an AI contract or approve an AI budget, we price it the way a CFO would: cost per task, cost at ten times the usage, what happens when the vendor reprices, and who carries the risk if it doesn’t deliver. [1 to 2] weeks. You get a one-page verdict and the questions to put to the vendor.

For: CFOs, procurement, and anyone holding an AI quote they can’t check.

Format: Fixed fee.

The Kitchen Cabinet

A standing adviser for your leadership team or board.

“Kitchen Cabinet” was the nickname for Andrew Jackson’s informal advisers: outside the official structure, and trusted for straight answers. That’s the job here. You get someone on call when a vendor pitch or a board question lands, plus a quarterly briefing on what’s changed in prices, rules and models.

For: Companies past their first plan.

Format: Monthly retainer. Minimum term: [minimum term].
