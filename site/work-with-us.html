Services

What we do for clients.

Three kinds of work, one skill: making AI make sense to the people who have to act on it. That might be you, your staff or your customers.

For your business

Find where AI fits

AI strategy for small and mid-sized companies.

You don’t need an AI strategy deck. You need to know which two or three jobs in your business AI would actually do well, what that would cost, and what to leave alone. We start with where your time and money leak, not with the tools, and work forward from there.

You get: A short, ranked list of where AI is worth trying, the order to try it in, and how you’ll know if it worked.

For: Owners and leadership teams who keep being told they need AI and want a straight answer about where.

For your people

Bring your people along

Messaging and training for a company rolling out AI.

Most AI rollouts stall on people, not software. Staff hear “we’re adopting AI” and wonder whether that means their job. We help you say plainly what is changing and why, set out what people can and can’t do with the tools, and teach them to use AI with their eyes open.

You get: The internal message, the plain-English guidance for staff, and training your people will sit through.

For: Leaders, HR and communications teams introducing AI tools to people who didn’t ask for them.

For your customers

Explain what you’ve built

Positioning and messaging for AI companies.

If you make an AI product, your buyer is probably not technical and has heard every claim before. We work out what your product does for that person, in words they would use, and help you say it without the hype. We write for the sceptical buyer because we are one, and we won’t write a claim you can’t support.

You get: A value proposition a non-technical buyer understands, and the core explanations of your product built on it.

For: AI companies whose product is better than their explanation of it.
