Country by country

The 33 countries we’ve profiled, from the ones leaning in hardest to the ones pushing back. It’s the same information as the globe, in a form you can read straight through.

Leaning in accelerating · 7

United Arab Emirates

All-in

Among the most AI-forward states on earth — it appointed the world’s first Minister of AI back in 2017.

Law to watch
National AI Strategy 2031; dedicated AI Minister
Good to know
Backing its own large models (Falcon) and enormous compute build-outs.

Singapore

Enabler

Pioneered practical, voluntary governance tools instead of heavy law — including open-source testing toolkit AI Verify.

Law to watch
Model AI Governance Framework (voluntary)
Good to know
Positioning itself as Asia’s trusted hub for AI governance.

Saudi Arabia

Investing big

Channeling sovereign wealth into AI as a pillar of economic diversification away from oil.

Law to watch
SDAIA national strategy
Good to know
Building national models and data centers at serious scale.

Japan

Soft-law, pro-AI

Favors voluntary guidelines over hard rules, and has unusually permissive copyright rules for training data.

Law to watch
Voluntary guidelines; permissive training-data rules
Good to know
Sees AI as a direct answer to a shrinking, aging workforce.

Israel

Innovation-first

Prefers applying existing sector rules to a dedicated AI law, protecting a famously dense startup scene.

Law to watch
Sector rules; light national policy
Good to know
One of the highest densities of AI startups per capita anywhere.

United Kingdom

Pro-innovation

Deliberately avoided an EU-style law. Instead it asks current regulators to apply five cross-sector principles — betting that flexibility beats a rulebook.

Law to watch
Principles-based, existing regulators (no single AI act)
Good to know
Hosted the first global AI Safety Summit and stood up an AI Safety Institute.

India

Pro-innovation

Has held back on hard regulation to avoid slowing growth, relying on light advisories rather than statute.

Law to watch
Advisories only — no binding AI law yet
Good to know
A global hub for AI talent and IT services delivery.

Warming up light-touch · 5

South Korea

Framework-led

Passed a comprehensive AI framework that promotes the industry while flagging "high-impact" uses for extra duties.

Law to watch
AI Basic Act
Good to know
Heavy government and chaebol investment in chips and home-grown models.

United States of America

Light-touch, fast-moving

No comprehensive federal AI law. Washington leans pro-innovation while individual states (Colorado, California, Texas) write their own rules — so the map inside the U.S. is itself a patchwork.

Law to watch
Executive orders + a patchwork of state laws
Good to know
Home to the largest AI labs and by far the deepest private AI investment on the planet.

Switzerland

Light-touch

Leaning on current law for now while it studies the EU AI Act and the Council of Europe’s AI treaty.

Law to watch
Existing law; watching the EU & Council of Europe
Good to know
Hosts top research (ETH Zurich) and big-tech AI labs.

China

State-directed

Pours state money into AI while tightly controlling outputs — public generative models need approval and must reflect "core socialist values".

Law to watch
Algorithm & generative-AI rules (Cyberspace Admin.)
Good to know
World's #2 for research output and a front-runner in deployment and surveillance.

Nigeria

Ambitious strategy

Launched one of Africa’s most ambitious national AI strategies, built around local talent.

Law to watch
National AI Strategy (2024)
Good to know
A fast-growing developer base centered on Lagos.

Cautious rules in progress · 14

Kenya

Emerging

Building an AI strategy on the back of its "Silicon Savannah" tech scene.

Law to watch
National AI strategy in draft

Australia

Tightening

Moving from voluntary guidance toward mandatory rules for "high-risk" AI settings.

Law to watch
Voluntary standard → mandatory guardrails proposed
Good to know
Strong applied focus on mining, agriculture and services.

South Africa

Developing

Developing a national framework that tries to balance growth with equity.

Law to watch
AI policy framework in progress
Good to know
The most developed tech sector on the continent.

Canada

Cautious, law pending

AIDA would regulate "high-impact" AI systems, but it stalled in Parliament — leaving Canada with intent but no binding law yet.

Law to watch
Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (proposed)
Good to know
Montreal and Toronto are world-class research hubs (the Bengio / Hinton lineage).

Egypt

Strategy-led

Has a national AI council and strategy aimed largely at public-sector modernization.

Law to watch
National AI Strategy; AI council

Indonesia

Pro-growth

Favoring guidelines over hard law as it courts digital investment.

Law to watch
Ethics guidelines; law under study
Good to know
Southeast Asia’s largest economy and internet market.

Norway

EU-aligned (EEA)

Not an EU member, but applies much of the EU digital rulebook — including the AI Act — through the EEA.

Law to watch
Adopts EU AI Act via the EEA

Russia

Sovereignty-focused

Pushes state-led "sovereign AI" for defense and administration, while sanctions throttle its access to advanced chips.

Law to watch
National AI strategy; state-led

Turkey

Strategy-led

Has a national strategy and is drafting a law, but nothing binding is in force yet.

Law to watch
National AI Strategy

Finland

Regulate-first (EU)

Implements the EU AI Act and has long invested in public AI literacy.

Law to watch
EU AI Act
Good to know
Its free "Elements of AI" course has been taken by millions worldwide.

Mexico

Early-stage

Debating national AI rules as nearshoring draws in AI-adjacent manufacturing investment.

Law to watch
No comprehensive law yet

Argentina

Early-stage

Has a national plan and recommendations, but no enforceable AI statute yet.

Law to watch
National AI plan; no binding law

Brazil

EU-inspired bill

Advancing a risk-based law modeled partly on the EU AI Act — the most developed AI legislation in Latin America.

Law to watch
AI Bill (PL 2338)
Good to know
The largest AI market on the continent.

Sweden

Regulate-first (EU)

Follows the EU AI Act while trying to keep its strong tech and gaming sectors competitive.

Law to watch
EU AI Act

Pushing back regulate first · 7

Ireland

Regulate-first (EU)

Under the EU AI Act, and a key enforcement venue since so many US tech firms base their EU HQ in Dublin.

Law to watch
EU AI Act

Spain

Regulate-first (EU)

Implements the EU AI Act and went further than most by creating a dedicated AI supervisory agency.

Law to watch
EU AI Act
Good to know
AESIA is one of Europe’s first standalone AI regulators.

France

Regulate-first (EU)

Bound by the EU AI Act — the world’s first comprehensive AI law — which bans some uses outright and polices "high-risk" ones. France pairs that with a hard push for homegrown champions.

Law to watch
EU AI Act (risk-tiered)
Good to know
Backing European challenger lab Mistral; positioning Paris as the EU AI capital.

Poland

Regulate-first (EU)

Bound by the EU AI Act; building out national supervisory capacity.

Law to watch
EU AI Act

Netherlands

Regulate-first (EU)

Applies the EU AI Act with extra wariness about automated decision-making in government.

Law to watch
EU AI Act
Good to know
A childcare-benefits algorithm scandal made it a cautionary tale for public-sector AI.

Italy

Regulate-first (EU)

Under the EU AI Act, and notably the first Western country to temporarily ban ChatGPT (2023) over privacy concerns.

Law to watch
EU AI Act + national AI bill
Good to know
Now advancing its own national law to sit alongside the EU rules.

Germany

Regulate-first (EU)

Implements the EU AI Act with a characteristically careful eye on data protection and the impact on industrial workers.

Law to watch
EU AI Act (risk-tiered)
Good to know
Focus is on trustworthy "industrial AI" for its manufacturing base.

AI rules are a moving target — the picture above is a plain-English snapshot, not legal advice. If you operate across borders and need to know what actually applies to you, that’s exactly the kind of thing we sort out in the consulting kitchen.
