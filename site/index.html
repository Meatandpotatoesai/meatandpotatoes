Where we stick our neck out

We think AI is going to need blockchain.

It’s not the take that gets you invited to parties, and we’ve written a lot about it. The short version: the smarter and more autonomous AI gets, the more it needs something models can’t provide on their own — a way to prove what’s true, who did what, and how the money moved. That’s a ledger problem. So when you see a run of blockchain pieces in the cookbook, that’s why.

01 — DATA INTEGRITY

A model is only as trustworthy as its data.

A tamper-evident ledger gives every training set, model, and output a chain of custody you can actually check — not just take on faith.

02 — TRUST

You can’t see inside the model, so verify around it.

Cryptographic proof beats “trust us” — for audit trails, consent, and settling who did what when it matters.

03 — AGENT ECONOMICS

Agents will transact with each other.

To pay, permission, and settle without a human in the loop, autonomous agents need identity and money that live on a shared ledger.
